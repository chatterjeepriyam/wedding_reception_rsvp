Getting There

How to Reach

✦
✈️
By Air
The nearest airport is Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport, Kolkata (CCU). From the airport, Serampore is approximately an hour and a half away by road, depending on traffic.
🚆
By Train
From Howrah Junction, board a local train on the main line towards Serampore or Rishra station. Both stations are well connected and within easy reach of the venue.
🛺
By Road
From Serampore or Rishra railway station, a short auto or toto ride will take you directly to the Mahesh SBI stoppage, right beside the venue.
